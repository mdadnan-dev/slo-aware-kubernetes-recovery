Across three randomized blocks, KM RMST(300) was 265.811 s for C2, 273.432 s for C5, and 267.698 s for PREWARM. Relative to C2, the mean paired differences were +7.621 s for C5 and +1.887 s for PREWARM; PREWARM minus C5 was -5.734 s. All treatments recovered in two of three episodes, with one right-censored episode per treatment. Exact sign-flip p-values were 0.50, 1.00, and 1.00, respectively. With only three pairs, the evaluation is exploratory and does not support a superiority or equivalence claim. PREWARM was descriptively close to C2 and faster than C5, but neither difference was statistically supported.
